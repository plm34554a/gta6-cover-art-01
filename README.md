Vice Panels is a small, fully offline poster cover maker that turns your own photos into a multi-panel collage in the style of a big-budget game cover. It is plain static web files under `app/` — no build step and no network calls — and your photos are read locally in the browser and never uploaded. You drop in one or more pictures, pick one of four slanted-panel collage layouts (Showcase, Grid Six, Skyline, or Split Diptych), then click a panel and drag or scroll to pan and zoom its photo into place; a single photo can also be spread across every panel as one continuous mosaic. A colour grade (Vice Sunset, Neon Night, Gold Hour, or Clean Print) is applied with an adjustable strength, and an editable title and tagline are drawn over the collage in bold poster lettering. The live preview and the final export share the same canvas renderer, so what you see is exactly what you get, and the finished cover downloads as a PNG at 1080×1080, 1080×1350, or 1920×1080.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Open the printed local address in a desktop browser to use the maker. Running `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts` produces the hand-in output (the app, the screenshots, and the dossier) in the chosen directory.
